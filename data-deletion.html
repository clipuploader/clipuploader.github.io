Data Deletion

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Clip Uploader stores no data on any server. All of its data (sign-in tokens and the videos you create) lives on your own computer, so you are always in control of deleting it.

1. Disconnect your accounts

  • YouTube / Google: open Google security settings, choose Clip Uploader → Remove access.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions → Clip Uploader → Remove access.

Removing access immediately stops Clip Uploader from posting and invalidates its tokens.

2. Delete local data

Delete the app's storage folder (or uninstall the app). This removes saved tokens, downloaded recordings and generated clips from your computer.

3. Videos already published

Videos uploaded to your accounts belong to you and stay on YouTube or TikTok until you delete them in YouTube Studio or the TikTok app.

4. Request confirmation

Email ala144hrz@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". We will reply within 30 days to confirm that no data related to you is held by us.
