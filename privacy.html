Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Last updated: October 10, 2026

In short: Clip Uploader is a desktop application that publishes videos you choose to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It requests only the permissions needed to publish, keeps sign-in tokens on your computer, has no servers or database, and never sells or shares your data.

1. Who we are

Clip Uploader ("the app", "we") is operated by its developer, reachable at ala144hrz@gmail.com. This policy applies to the desktop application and to this website.

2. YouTube API Services

Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the YouTube channel you connect. By using Clip Uploader you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access

SourceWhat is accessedWhy
Google / YouTube Permission to upload videos to your channel (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload). The app does not read your channel's videos, comments, subscriptions, watch history, email or contacts. To upload the videos you choose, with their title, description, tags and privacy setting.
TikTok Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic; the creator settings TikTok returns before posting (allowed privacy levels, comment/duet/stitch availability); permission to publish videos (video.publish). To confirm which account a video will be posted to and to publish the videos you choose with settings TikTok allows.
Your computer Video files and captions you create with the app. To produce and upload your clips. These files never leave your computer except when uploaded to your own accounts.

4. How we use information

Information is used only to publish videos to the accounts you connected and to show you the result. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose, and it is not used to train machine-learning models.

5. Storage and security

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in local files on your computer.
  • Clip Uploader has no servers and no database; we never receive or store your tokens or videos.
  • All communication with Google and TikTok uses HTTPS.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to Google (YouTube) and TikTok, and only as needed to publish your videos.

7. Cookies

The app does not use cookies. This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages and does not set cookies or use analytics; GitHub may process basic request logs as described in its own privacy statement.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or delete them; disconnecting removes the app's access immediately.
  • Because no data is stored on our side, there is nothing for us to retain. If you contact us with a deletion request, we will confirm within 30 days that no data related to you is held by us.
  • Step-by-step instructions are on the Data Deletion page.

9. Revoking access

You can revoke Clip Uploader's access at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: visit the Google security settings page, select Clip Uploader and choose "Remove access".
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions, select Clip Uploader and remove access.

10. Children

Clip Uploader is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and does not knowingly collect their information.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold your data, these rights are exercised directly by revoking access and deleting local files; you can also contact us for confirmation.

12. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes will be highlighted here before they take effect.

13. Contact

Email ala144hrz@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.
