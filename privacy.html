Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Clip Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the connected channel (youtube.upload scope). The app does not read your viewing history, subscriptions, comments or other channel data.
  • TikTok: basic account information (such as display name and avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).

How the data is used

Access is used only to publish videos that the owner has created and chosen to upload, with their titles, captions and privacy settings. Nothing else is done with it.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no servers and no database, and does not send your data to anyone other than Google (YouTube) and TikTok, as required to publish.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token files also disconnects the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used to collect data from them.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
