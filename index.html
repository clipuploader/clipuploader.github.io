Desktop app · Private beta

Turn long livestreams into ready-to-post short videos.

Clip Uploader finds the most engaging moments in a stream recording, edits them into vertical clips with captions, and publishes them to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts through each platform's official API.

How it works

Clip Uploader runs on the creator's own computer. Nothing is published to an account until its owner has connected it and chosen the publishing settings.

1

Connect your accounts

Sign in with Google (YouTube) and TikTok using their official sign-in pages. You see exactly which permissions are requested and can revoke them at any time.

2

Find the moments

The app analyses a stream recording (audio energy and speech) to suggest the strongest 15–45 second moments.

3

Edit automatically

Each moment becomes a 9:16 vertical clip with face-following framing, readable captions and a title.

4

Publish on your schedule

Clips are uploaded to your connected accounts with your title, caption, hashtags and the privacy level you choose, a few times per day.

Supported platforms

Clip Uploader only uses official, documented APIs.

▶️

YouTube

Uploads Shorts with the YouTube Data API v3 (youtube.upload scope only). Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services; by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

🎵

TikTok

Publishes videos with the TikTok Content Posting API after you sign in with Login Kit (user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). Your TikTok account name and the privacy options TikTok allows are checked before each post.

Your data, your control

Clip Uploader is built to need as little access as possible.

  • No servers, no database: sign-in tokens are stored only on your own computer.
  • Only the permissions needed to publish your videos are requested; nothing is read from your inbox, contacts or viewing history.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data.
  • Disconnect at any time from your Google account permissions or TikTok's app permissions; see Data deletion.
