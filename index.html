Clip Uploader

Turns long livestream recordings into vertical clips, and posts them to your own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Open Studio Privacy Policy
  1. Find the momentsThe app reads a recording's audio and speech and suggests its strongest 15–40 second moments.
  2. Edit the clipEach moment becomes a vertical video with face-following framing and captions.
  3. Review and postIn the Studio you check the clip, caption and privacy, then post it yourself.

Platforms

YouTube

Uploads Shorts with the YouTube Data API (youtube.upload only). By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

TikTok

Signs in with Login Kit and posts with the Content Posting API. Nothing is sent to TikTok until you click Post.

Your data

Sign-ins and videos stay on your computer. There are no servers, ads or analytics. You can disconnect any account at any time; see Data deletion.
