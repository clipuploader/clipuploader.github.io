Clip Uploader

A personal publishing tool for short-form videos.

Clip Uploader is a small private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It edits short vertical videos and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts, using each platform's official API.

It is not a public service: it has no user sign-ups, does not access anyone else's accounts, and does not sell, share or analyse personal data.

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files and their captions to the connected account.
  • Reads the minimum account information needed to publish (for example, the account name).
  • Stores sign-in tokens only on the owner's computer.
